# Store page: Spear-Mace: Sky Arena

Everything to paste into the CrazyGames developer portal (it also works for Poki and itch.io).

## Game title
Spear-Mace: Sky Arena

## Short description (one line)
Dash with a wind spear, slam down from the clouds with a storm mace, and knock your rivals off floating sky islands.

## Long description
Spear-Mace: Sky Arena is a fast 3D arena brawler set on islands floating above the clouds.

Your **Wind Spear** dashes wherever you look, on the ground or in mid-air, and pierces straight through rivals.
A hit at full reach is a critical. Your **Storm Mace** rewards daring: the higher you fall, the harder the slam.
A hit bounces you back into the sky for another one. Ride the wind geyser to the floating monoliths, glide,
double-jump with a wind burst, and use the jump pads to fly between islands. Parry at the last moment to stun
your attacker, or send them into the void below.

**Modes**
- Quick Play: a duel in one click
- Duel: 1v1 against Easy, Normal, Pro or God bots
- Brawl: a free-for-all against three bots
- King of the Hill: hold the glowing zone to score
- Last Stand: two lives each, last fighter standing wins
- Gauntlet: one life against endless waves of tougher bots
- Training: a short tutorial and practice dummy

**Progression:** 8 classes with their own powers, unlocked as you level up (Shadow, Lightning, Energy, Potionmaster, Pyro,
Void Walker, Buddha and Normal), skins and weapon glows to buy with gold, daily missions and arena ranks from
Bronze to Obsidian. There are two arenas, the Sky Colosseum and the Sky Ruins, at sunset, under the stars or in a thunderstorm.

Play in first person or over the shoulder (V), with mouse and keyboard, a gamepad or touch controls.

## Controls
| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD / mouse | Left / right stick | Left thumb / drag right side |
| Jump, wind jump (press again), glide (hold) | Space | A | JUMP |
| Spear dash where you look | Left click | RT, RB or X | SPEAR |
| Mace slam (in the air) / swing (on the ground) | Right click or Shift | LT or B | MACE |
| Parry | F | LB | PARRY |
| Switch first / third person | V | Back | 👁 |
| Pause | P or Esc | Start | ❚❚ |
